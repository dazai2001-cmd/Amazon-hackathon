TITLE
Earned Valor | Fitness, Adventure & Friendly Competition on Fire TV

DESCRIPTION
Train your body. Forge your legend.

Earned Valor brings fitness, adventure and friendly competition to the living-room TV. Train with a medieval mentor, grow your squire through completed routines, enjoy a voiced story and put movement and timing into sword-and-shield duels.

Our goal is to give families and friends a reason to move, play and return together. The working prototype supports personal progression and local/LAN clan matches across independent screens and controllers. Training XP provides bounded story bonuses; competitive clan duels use common starter gear without those bonuses.

Our longer-term vision is online play with family and friends across households, followed by opt-in matches against new rivals and clans. Internet hosting, account-backed clans, public matchmaking and rankings are planned. The footage demonstrates the current local prototype.

This video records the native Android TV APK in an emulator. A synthetic skeleton sends pose landmarks through the real relay and game rules; its side panel visualizes the same recorded pose stream. Clan Wars uses two scripted controllers, with the server resolving health, the result and the clan score. No live phone-camera footage or human exercise accuracy is demonstrated. Training/rest are shortened in the edit, and progression belongs to an isolated test profile.

The recording does not measure camera-to-display latency or physical Fire TV performance. It includes offline synthetic narration and the project's original music. Asset and tool attribution is in THIRD_PARTY.md.
